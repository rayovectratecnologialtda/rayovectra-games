Privacy policy · Iron Carnage

Updated November 14, 2026

This policy explains how Rayovectra Tecnologia LTDA handles data to operate the game and provide support.

Data and purposes

Identity and access
Your email, account identifier and player name are used to authenticate you, confirm your account and identify your profile.
Matches and progress
Match data, rank, trophies, garage, arsenal, items and in-game transactions may be linked to your profile to run online modes, save progress, display rankings and protect the service.
Support
We use your address, message and the details you include to answer your request and investigate issues.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, account services, storage and online match services. The stores handle payments for store purchases.

Providers that process data on Rayovectra’s behalf must protect it to the standard described in this policy and use it for the contracted services.

Retention and deletion

Account and progression data are used while your account is active. When you request deletion, you can ask us to remove your profile and associated data. Records needed to comply with the law, document transactions or prevent fraud are kept for the relevant period. We will explain any records that must be kept and the applicable period when responding to your request.

Your data and choices

Use the email address below to request access, correction, deletion or information about how we use your data. We may verify you own the account before acting.

Visiting this website

The information pages are static. The hosting provider may process connection and request details to deliver and protect the pages. Contact buttons open your email app, where you can review and send the message.
