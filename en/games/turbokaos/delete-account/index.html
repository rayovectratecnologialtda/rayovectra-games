Request account deletion · TURBOKAOS

Email us a deletion request. Include your player name and the email address linked to the game if you know it. If you used a private address to sign in, send the request from an address you can access. Rayovectra may verify account ownership before processing deletion.

Data associated with the account

The account profile, player name, garage progress, items, coins, trophies, results and race times linked to your account.

Deletion closes access to the profile and can erase its progress, items and results. Uninstalling the game leaves the account on the server. Manage any active store subscription directly with the store.

Retention and deletion

Account and progression data are used while your account is active. When you request deletion, you can ask us to remove your profile and associated data. Records needed to comply with the law, document transactions or prevent fraud are kept for the relevant period. We will explain any records that must be kept and the applicable period when responding to your request.
