Privacy policy · TURBOKAOS

Updated November 15, 2026

This policy explains how Rayovectra Tecnologia LTDA handles data to operate the game and provide support.

Data and purposes

Identity and access
Your account identifier, email received at sign-in and player name are used to authenticate you, recover access and link your profile to progress.
Matches and progress
We keep information such as results, times, trophies, garage items, currencies and in-game purchases to sync progress, run matches and prevent fraud. Your player name and best times may appear on leaderboards.
Support
We use your address, message and the details you choose to include to answer your request and investigate issues.
Rewarded ads
In mobile versions with this feature enabled, Google Mobile Ads provides optional ads and reward verification. The integration may process IP addresses, device details, identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. The age range you provide for ad settings is saved on your device. When available in your version and region, open Settings and select Privacy to review your choices.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, profile storage and match services. Google and Apple process sign-in when you choose them. Google also provides ads in versions with the feature enabled. The stores handle payments for store purchases.

Providers that process data on Rayovectra’s behalf must protect it to the standard described in this policy and use it for the contracted services.

Data protection

Connections to the account service use HTTPS. Access to your profile and progress is linked to your authenticated identity.

Retention and deletion

Account and progression data are used while your account is active. When you request deletion, you can ask us to remove your profile and associated data. Records needed to comply with the law, document transactions or prevent fraud are kept for the relevant period. We will explain any records that must be kept and the applicable period when responding to your request.

Your data and choices

Use the privacy contact to request access, correction, deletion or information about how we use your data. You may also ask to withdraw consent. We may verify account ownership before acting.

Visiting this website

GitHub Pages hosts this site and logs IP addresses for security. Contact buttons open your email app, where you can review and send the message. Privacy on GitHub Pages
